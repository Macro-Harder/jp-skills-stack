# Pre-Plan

## What it is
Pre-plan is the scoping stage that runs at the front of a build, before the grill, and again before the design plan. Its purpose is simple: no design should be committed until the work, its options, and its open questions are understood well enough to ask good questions about them.

Three groups do the work together. A research team studies the subject in parallel, with each member taking a deliberately different approach, perspective, or goal. An imagination team works alongside, producing alternative ways to frame the problem, novel approaches, what if variants, and radical simpler cuts. A clerk keeps one shared evidence register that both teams feed.

The register is the heart of the stage. It separates what is established from what is merely repeated and from what is still open. When the stage closes, the register holds a scope statement, the options with their trade offs, and the open questions in plain language. That register is what the grill works from when it interviews the owner and settles what is being built, what done looks like, and what stays out of scope.

## Example
A team is considering a new tool that checks documents for missing citations. Before anyone designs it, pre-plan runs.

One researcher maps how citation checking works today and what tools already exist. Another studies failure cases, where checkers miss real gaps or flag correct work. A third looks at cost and speed limits. The imagination team proposes other framings: a checker that only flags high risk sections, a version that works live while a document is written, and a much simpler version that only checks reference lists.

The clerk records that existing tools handle formatted reference lists well, a point three papers agree on, and that accuracy on in text citations is disputed, with two sources giving different results. Open questions include which document types are in scope and what error rate is acceptable. The grill then settles those questions with the owner before any brief is written.

## How to use it
Place this folder in the skills directory so an agent can load SKILL.md when a build is starting or when a design plan is about to be written. The person starting the work supplies the subject and why it matters. The coordinator seats the research team, the imagination team, and the clerk, then runs the stage to the closing test in the procedure.

The only required output is the evidence register. Keep it in the project records so the grill and later design work can build on it.

## For agents
An agent running pre-plan protects the separation between proposals and decisions. Imagination output is logged as proposals and lands in a brief or plan only after audit passes it. The clerk agent updates the register as each paper lands, not at the end. A coordinating agent does not close the stage until the register carries the scope, the options, and the open questions in a form the grill can use without going back to raw papers.
