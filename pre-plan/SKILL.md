# Pre-Plan

This skill distils Step 3, Pre-plan, in LEVEL-2-STEPS.md of the build flow documents. Where this skill and the source disagree, the source wins.

## Purpose
Pre-plan runs before the grill, and again before the design plan. Its job is to scope the work, surface the real options, and state the open questions plainly, before anyone commits to a design. It replaces early guessing with evidence gathered from several angles at once.

## Procedure
1. State the subject in one short brief: what is being considered, why it matters, and what a useful outcome of this stage looks like.
2. Seat a research team to work the subject in parallel. Give members deliberately different approaches, perspectives, and goals, so the same ground is not covered the same way twice.
3. Seat an imagination team alongside the research team. Its work is to produce alternative framings, novel approaches, what if variants, cross domain analogies, ambitious versions, and radical simpler cuts. Treat all of its output as proposals, never as decisions.
4. Appoint a clerk before work starts. The clerk keeps one shared evidence register for the whole stage and updates it as papers land.
5. Record in the register three things, kept separate: what is established, with its source; what is duplicated across teams, so agreement is visible; and what is still open, stated as a question that can be answered.
6. Have the clerk reconcile conflicts in the register. Where sources disagree, note both positions and what would settle the matter. Do not smooth disagreement away.
7. Close the stage only when the register can carry the grill. That means it holds a clear scope statement, the options with their trade offs, and the open questions stated plainly.
8. Hand the register to the grill. The grill interviews the owner until what is being built, what done looks like, and what is out of scope are decided. Run pre-plan again, at the same depth, before the design plan is written.

## Edge cases
- If research members return the same findings, the perspectives were not different enough. Reseat with sharper differences and run the gap again.
- If imagination output starts being treated as decided, stop and move it back to proposals in the register.
- If the register has no open questions, treat that as a warning sign. Look harder before closing.
- Never close the stage on raw team output alone. The register is the deliverable.
- Never let one strong paper stand in for the whole register.
- Never start design work inside pre-plan.
