# Team Staffing

Quick version, written 2026-11-14 outside the build flow. The full team and member definitions live in ~/workspace/build-flow/LEVEL-3-TEAMS.md and LEVEL-4-MEMBERS.md, with tier role files under ~/workspace/build-flow-agents/. This skill is the loadable short form. Where this skill and the documents disagree, the documents win.

Use this skill whenever a team is seated for any stage of a build.

## The ladder

Team sizes set by Captain JP, by tier, in the order Low, Medium, High, Extra High, Plaid. These are minimums, never ceilings, and never reduced.

- Note-taking: 4, 4, 6, 8, 10
- Pre-plan research: 5, 9, 15, 25, 35
- Research: 3, 6, 12, 24, 48
- Imagination: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10
- Design: 2, 3, 4, 8, 12
- Build: 3, 5, 7, 11, 15
- Audit, per audit: 3, 9, 21, 35, 35
- Logic audit, per loop: 5, 15, 25, 35, 35
- Review board: at least 3 at every tier, sitting at every step

A size change governs every stage not yet closed. Stages already closed keep the staffing they closed with.

## The rules

- A member is a distinct agent. One agent never wears several hats or covers two seats. The single exception is the small tier's audit team of at least 3 wearing several colour lenses.
- Agents are a fluid pool of unique roles, divided by family: builders are seated only from the builder pool, auditors only from the auditor pool, and so on. Fluidity runs inside a family and across steps and rounds, never across families. The Manager picks the members a plan calls for, best fitted to the work.
- Carried seats sit on top of the base numbers: every design and build team carries a fold-in tracer and a principles agent as distinct seats; every design team carries a contrarian; every build carries a live-proof owner from mid-build on; parallel research carries a clerk keeping the shared evidence register.
- Any agent directly modifying code sits in a build team, never solo, and each build team member builds from a different perspective.
- A stage that ran short on distinct agents does not count as staffed. Restaff it for any work not yet closed, and state the roster, stage by stage, in the report.
- Five warnings each stop the step: a standing role has nobody in it; the danger list is out of date; a record list was never converted into the system; a role is filled but the person only watches and does no work; the practice drill for attacking the gates was missed.
