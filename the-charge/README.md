# The Charge

## What it is

This skill covers the written charge that opens an audit or review round. The charge is agreed before any examination begins, by the producing team, the Project Manager, and the Auditor as chair. It is the fixed reference point for the whole round.

The charge names five things: the scope of the round, its success criteria, its agenda, the audit criteria it will judge against, and the board published list of the materials it will inspect. With those five settled in advance, the round knows what it is looking at, what counts as success, and what standard each finding will be judged by.

Every verdict the round issues must cite the charge it was issued under. That citation is what lets a later reader trace a verdict back to the exact terms in force when the work was examined.

The charge can change during a round, but only in limited ways. The chair may amend scope and roster, and each amendment and its reason is recorded. The audit criteria and the success criteria are not amendable once examination has begun. If either needs to change, the correct move is a new charge and a new round. No amendment applies retroactively, so work examined under an earlier scope is reported under that scope, not quietly re-labelled under the new one.

Agreeing a charge is a narrow act. It fixes scope and criteria. It does not approve staffing in advance, veto who may be staffed, or act as a condition that a spin-up must satisfy before it can work.

## Example

Before a review of a payment module, the producing team, the Project Manager, and the Auditor agree a charge. Scope is the payment module and its tests, not the wider billing system. Success criteria state what a passing round looks like. The agenda orders the sessions. Audit criteria list the standards for correctness, security, and record keeping that findings will cite. The materials list names the code snapshot, the test results, and the design notes to be inspected. Mid round, a new reviewer joins and scope is widened to include a small adapter, with the amendment and its reason recorded. Findings on the original module are still reported under the original scope.

## How to use it

Supply the round, the parties, and drafts of the five charge elements. The charge is agreed and recorded before examination starts. Anyone using this skill during a round checks verdicts against the cited charge and checks any amendment against the limits on what may be amended.

## For agents

An agent must not begin examination before the charge is agreed, must cite the charge on every verdict it contributes to, and must refuse to treat a mid round change to audit criteria or success criteria as an amendment. That change needs a new charge and a new round. An agent also must not use the charge as a staffing gate or as a precondition for a spin-up.
