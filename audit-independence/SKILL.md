# Audit Independence

This skill distils rule R-71, Independence as threat elimination, in LEVEL-3-TEAMS.md of the build flow documents. Where this skill and the source disagree, the source wins.

## Purpose

An auditor must be independent in fact and in appearance. Independence is not shown by stating good faith. It is shown by naming the threats that could compromise judgement and giving each one a clear disposition before examination starts.

## Procedure

1. Open the round with the charge step. The Auditor, acting as chair, leads this step.
2. Name all five threats for this engagement: self-interest, self-review, advocacy, familiarity, and intimidation.
3. Give each threat exactly one disposition: eliminated, reduced to an acceptable level by a named safeguard, or the engagement is declined.
4. Treat self-review as prohibited. No safeguard cures it. If self-review is present and cannot be eliminated, decline the engagement.
5. Do not list good faith as a safeguard. A safeguard must be a concrete measure that can be checked.
6. Write the result as a threat naming record inside the round record. List each threat, its disposition, and the safeguard relied on where one applies.
7. Stop. No audit work counts toward the round until that record exists.
8. Send every disposition other than self-review for checking by a verifier outside the assessed team.
9. Record the verifier judgement with the disposition it checks. The acceptable level is that verifier judgement, not the audit team view.
10. Treat any disposition with no outside check recorded as not standing. Complete the check before the record stands.

## Edge cases

If a threat emerges mid round, pause work, add it to the record, assign a disposition, and get it checked before resuming.

If the only verifier available sits inside the assessed team, the check does not count. Find an outside verifier or leave the disposition unstanding.

If intimidation or self-interest cannot be reduced to a level the outside verifier accepts, decline. A partial audit under a live threat is worse than no audit.
